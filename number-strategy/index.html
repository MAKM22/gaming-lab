How to play & prototype choices
The numbers behind the idea
Play on your phone or offline

This is a web prototype, not an App Store release. When served over HTTPS (or localhost), open it in your phone browser and use its Install app or Add to Home Screen menu. Availability depends on the browser. The app reports when its offline files are ready. On iPhone, use Safari's Share menu. Saves live on this device and browser only; private browsing or clearing site data can erase them. No accounts, network opponents, or tracking.

Direct-file desktop play also works by opening index.html; installation and offline caching require a web server. The printable kit is an independent way to play without a device.
