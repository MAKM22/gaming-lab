# Gaming Lab / 144 number strategy
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Users and Purpose
Casual players sharing a phone or physical tabletop, confirmed by the user. Make
the number-sequence idea playable as both a local two-player app and a paper game.
Preserve Gaming Lab's broader notebook purpose.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Confirmed: 12 by 12 board, 162 physical pieces, alternating placements or
one-square moves; a player's ordered 3-6-9, 1-5-7, or 2-4-8 straight line wins.
Touch and keyboard access, printable assets and concise rules are required.
Prototype defaults: 81 pieces per player, nine copies of each digit, diagonal
adjacency, no capture, both reading directions, no board wrapping. No backend,
accounts, deployment, App Store release or claims of proven balance.

## Evidence on Hand
The user's mathematical catalogue is context, not automatically a ruleset.
Keep governors and other constants in a separate reference, without physics claims.

## Product Principles
- Share identical rules between the screen and table.
- Distinguish player ownership without relying on color.
- Make selections, illegal actions, end states and storage failures explicit.
- Label invented prototype decisions rather than attributing them to the user.
